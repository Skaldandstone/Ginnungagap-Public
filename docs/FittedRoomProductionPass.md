# Fitted room production pass

The production-dressing and systems-binding pass is generated by
`tools/fit_modular_rooms_to_ship_levels.py`.

Actions 161–660 are tracked in `docs/RoomSystemsImplementationLedger.md`. Every fitted room now
owns a bounded gameplay profile, replicated operational state, typed gameplay anchors, and direct
bindings to its identity light and signage.
It owns actors labeled `ModularFit_*`, leaving hand-authored actors untouched. Every fitted room
receives two function-specific machinery silhouettes, a terminal, code/name signage, a colored
utility light, three gameplay anchors, and two hazard floor markers.

## Concept-matched CRYO-01 module

The dedicated cryo module is rebuilt by `tools/build_cryo_room_blender.py` from the repository-owned
reference at `docs/concept-art/reference/rooms/cryo-awakening-compact-damaged-concept.png`. The current
revision is a compact 9.0 x 5.4 x 3.2 m compartment with four one-sided pods, a clear circulation
aisle, and two increasingly damaged rear pods. Its stable bulkhead, system, loot, maintenance,
entry, and AI anchors remain part of the FBX output contract.

Run `tools/import_cryo_room_assets.py` through UnrealEditor-Cmd after rebuilding. It imports the
combined shell and machinery meshes to `/Game/Assets/ShipRooms/Cryo`, retaining the authored
materials and generating collision and lightmap UVs. Then run `tools/validate_cryo_room_assets.py`
through UnrealEditor-Cmd; it rejects missing assets, incorrect compact-room bounds, lost material
slots, or invalid lightmap-channel settings.

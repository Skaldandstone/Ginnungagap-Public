# Concept Ship Remaster V03 - Distributed Propulsion

V03 keeps the clean concept-authority hulls from V02 and redesigns propulsion on the two capital ships so their silhouettes and thrust loads spread laterally instead of terminating in a single engine wall.

| Ship | Central drives | Wing drives | Total | Wing role |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Small Utility / Escort | 6 | 0 | 6 | unchanged compact utility hull |
| Medium Military Corvette | 6 | 2 per side | 10 | swept armored propulsion wings with structural roots |
| Large Expedition Carrier | 6 | 3 per side | 12 | long swept propulsion wings with armored trusses and radiator mounts |

All ships retain their concept dimensions: 900 × 125 × 250 m, 2,400 × 430 × 620 m, and 6,500 × 1,400 × 1,800 m.

## Paths

- Source packages and renders: `Art/Ships/Exterior/ConceptRemasterV03/<Ship>/`
- Unreal meshes: `/Game/Assets/Ships/Exterior/ConceptRemasterV03/<Ship>/`
- Unreal review maps: `/Game/Assets/Maps/ShipExterior/ConceptRemasterV03/`
- Import report: `Saved/Reports/ConceptShipRemasterV03Import.json`

V03 is isolated for visual comparison and does not overwrite V02 or production fleet assets.

# Ginnungagap Ship Concept Art

The original kilometer-scale exterior set established the three ship envelopes and material
language. Its symmetric propulsion layouts and laterally loaded silhouettes are superseded for new
work by [Ship Architecture Authority](../../ShipArchitectureAuthority.md).

| Class | Role | Dimensions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small | Utility / Escort | 1.4 km long, 260 m beam, 320 m high |
| Medium | Military Corvette | 2.4 km long, 430 m beam, 620 m high |
| Large | Expedition Carrier | 6.5 km long, 1.4 km beam, 1.8 km high |

The retained industrial language is worn off-white composite armor, gunmetal structure,
ceramic-black thermal surfaces, muted safety-orange identification, recessed hangars, protected
drive districts, and deep-space-only construction. New ships align their tower-like deck stack
with the thrust axis, use transverse floors facing the aft engine base, and combine asymmetric
functional massing with unequal engine modules, calm
armor fields, and substantially less exterior protrusion clutter.

The Small Utility Escort gameplay specification supersedes the dimensions and population text printed on its original concept sheet. Its complete assembled bounds are 1,400 × 260 × 320 m, including drives, docking hardware, and command/sensor equipment. It contains approximately 24–32 operational decks and supports no more than 1,000 crew at full complement; much of the hull volume is machinery, tanks, armor, hangars, structural void, or robotic service space rather than conventional decks.

The earlier meter-scale ship sheets and the symmetric geometry in this set remain preserved as
lineage, but they are not current construction authority.

## Internal blueprints

The internal sheets are district-level planning documents rather than literal drawings of every deck. Each combines a longitudinal cutaway, representative deck or sector plan, circulation, pressure zoning, and Bloom containment infrastructure.

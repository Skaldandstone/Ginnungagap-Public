The sheets are production direction, not finished Unreal or Blender assets. Approved facts and
provisional visual suggestions are separated in each JSON packet.

Recursive source sets preserve complete concept lineages without treating every intermediate render
as a separate shipping asset. Rejected and superseded art remains inventoried and linked with an
explicit lifecycle. Complete inventory linkage is not production-ready status.

Helmet HUD v2 supersedes v1. V1 incorrectly gave fabricated Bloom telemetry a unique color and
label. V2 routes genuine and fabricated Bloom readings through the same player-facing warning
language so the player receives no truth leak.

Shared Pressure Suit v2 supersedes v1 because v1 drifted from the project suit concepts and its
first-person inset contained invalid anatomy. Traversal and Performance v2 supersedes the older
sixteen-pose sheet. The canonical playable role list is Science, Engineering, Medical, and Security
/ Recovery. Crew is a collective noun, not a class. Technician and Marine remain legacy art aliases
for Engineering and Security respectively.
